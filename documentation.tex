\section*{Purpose}
This document specifies (i) a GICS-like thematic taxonomy (``TICS'') with definitions and example constituents, and (ii) a financial knowledge graph (KG) schema---entity types and relationships---suitable for automated triple extraction from news/research. It also formalizes a graph-based assignment method that builds theme indices from the \emph{last $X$ days} (e.g., 30) of temporal KGs using path-distance to theme nodes.

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Notation:} Companies indexed by $i\in\mathcal{C}$, themes by $k\in\mathcal{T}$, days by $t$. The rolling window length is $X$ days.

\section{Theme Definitions (TICS L1) with Example Stocks}
Three liquid, illustrative examples per theme (not investment advice).

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Compute \& Digital Infrastructure}\\
\emph{Definition:} Firms that design, build, or operate the hardware, software, and facilities powering large-scale compute, networking, storage, and edge.\\
\emph{Examples:} NVIDIA (NVDA), Broadcom (AVGO), Equinix (EQIX).

\item \textbf{Energy \& Power Systems}\\
\emph{Definition:} Generation, transmission, distribution, and conversion technologies/services supplying reliable electrical power on- and off-grid.\\
\emph{Examples:} Schneider Electric (SU FP), Eaton (ETN), NextEra Energy (NEE).

\item \textbf{Materials \& Critical Resources}\\
\emph{Definition:} Producers and processors of metals, minerals, chemicals, and recycling essential to electrification, compute, mobility, and clean energy supply chains.\\
\emph{Examples:} Freeport-McMoRan (FCX), Cameco (CCJ), Albemarle (ALB).

\item \textbf{Mobility \& Logistics}\\
\emph{Definition:} Vehicles, components, charging/fueling, autonomy/ADAS, robotics, and logistics networks enabling movement of people and goods.\\
\emph{Examples:} Tesla (TSLA), BYD (1211 HK), DSV (DSV DC).

\item \textbf{Health \& Human Performance}\\
\emph{Definition:} Biopharma, medtech, diagnostics, and digital health improving longevity, metabolic/chronic-care outcomes, and real-time patient monitoring.\\
\emph{Examples:} Novo Nordisk (NOVO-B DC), Eli Lilly (LLY), Dexcom (DXCM).

\item \textbf{Security, Defense \& Resilience}\\
\emph{Definition:} Defense primes/suppliers, cyber and critical-infrastructure security, and resilience technologies for contested/disrupted environments.\\
\emph{Examples:} BAE Systems (BA/ LN), Lockheed Martin (LMT), RTX (RTX).

\item \textbf{Financial Infrastructure \& Digital Assets}\\
\emph{Definition:} Payments rails, market plumbing, custody/compliance, and tokenization platforms enabling real-time, interoperable finance.\\
\emph{Examples:} Adyen (ADYEN NA), Fiserv (FI), Coinbase (COIN).

\item \textbf{Climate Adaptation, Water \& Environment}\\
\emph{Definition:} Water treatment/desalination, monitoring/metering, environmental services and adaptation solutions mitigating physical climate risks.\\
\emph{Examples:} Xylem (XYL), Ecolab (ECL), Veolia (VIE FP).

\item \textbf{Built Environment \& Industrial Systems}\\
\emph{Definition:} Automation, controls, building systems, and industrial technologies that raise productivity, safety, and energy efficiency.\\
\emph{Examples:} Siemens (SIE GR), Rockwell Automation (ROK), Honeywell (HON).

\item \textbf{Consumer, Media \& Experiences}\\
\emph{Definition:} Platforms and brands monetizing attention and discretionary spend via streaming, interactive content, creator ecosystems, and premium goods.\\
\emph{Examples:} Netflix (NFLX), Roblox (RBLX), LVMH (MC FP).

\item \textbf{Space \& Frontier Technologies}\\
\emph{Definition:} Launch, LEO communications, Earth observation, and enabling components/data platforms extending operations beyond Earth.\\
\emph{Examples:} Rocket Lab (RKLB), Iridium (IRDM), SES (SESG LX).

\item \textbf{Government, Education \& Public Tech}\\
\emph{Definition:} Software/services tailored to public sector and education: identity, case management, citizen services, and analytics.\\
\emph{Examples:} Palantir (PLTR), Tyler Technologies (TYL), Pearson (PSON LN).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Extraction Guidance (for LLM prompts).}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item Emit normalized triples with \texttt{(head, relation, tail, time, source, confidence)}.
\item Head/tail must be typed to the schema above; if ambiguous, attach \texttt{alias} and \texttt{context} fields.
\item Prefer \texttt{about} over \texttt{mentions} when the document's main subject is clear.
\item When extracting \texttt{Metric}, include \texttt{value}, \texttt{unit}, \texttt{period} (ISO date), and \texttt{provenance} (quote span).
\item Map Themes using the fixed L1 enum; L2 is free-form but mapped to parent L1.
\end{itemize}

\section{Temporal KG \& Path-Distance Assignment}

\subsection{Rolling Temporal Graph}
For day $t$, construct a time-sliced, heterogeneous KG $G_t=(V_t,E_t)$ from documents in the window $[t-X+1,\,t]$. Each edge $e=(u\xrightarrow{r}v)$ carries a weight
\[
w_{e} \;=\; q_{\text{src}} \cdot s_{\text{extract}} \cdot \exp(-\lambda\,\Delta t_e),
\]
where $q_{\text{src}}\in[0,1]$ encodes source quality, $s_{\text{extract}}\in[0,1]$ extraction confidence, and $\Delta t_e$ is age in days.

\subsection{Typed Shortest-Path Distance}
Let $\mathcal{V}_\mathcal{C}$ be company nodes and $\mathcal{V}_\mathcal{T}$ the L1 theme nodes. Define a cost for traversing edge $e$:
\[
c(e) \;=\; -\log\!\big(\min(w_{e},\,1-\varepsilon)\big) + \gamma \,\Omega(r_e),
\]
where $\Omega(r)$ is a nonnegative penalty by relation type (e.g., lower for \texttt{about}, \texttt{supplies\_to}; higher for generic \texttt{mentions}), and $\gamma\ge 0$. For a company $i$ and theme $k$, define the (typed) shortest-path distance
\[
d_t(i,k) \;=\; \min_{\pi\in \Pi(i\to k)} \sum_{e\in \pi} c(e),
\]
with $\Pi(i\to k)$ the set of admissible meta-paths (e.g., $C\!\to\!N\!\to\!T$, $C\!\to\!C\!\to\!N\!\to\!T$, $C\!\to\!T$ if asserted).

\subsection{Theme Universe by Distance Threshold}
For theme $k$, define the investable \emph{universe candidate set} at $t$:
\[
\mathcal{U}_k(t;\tau_d, L_{\max}) \;=\; \left\{\, i\in\mathcal{V}_\mathcal{C}\;:\; d_t(i,k)\le \tau_d \ \ \land\ \ \exists \pi\in\Pi(i\to k)\text{ with }|\pi|\le L_{\max}\, \right\}.
\]
Typical values: $X=30$ days, $L_{\max}\in\{2,3,4\}$, $\tau_d$ tuned to target hit-rate/purity.

\subsection{Link Strength (for ranking/weights)}
Convert distances to strengths with a calibrated link:
\[
\ell_t(i,k) \;=\; \sigma\!\big(a - b\, d_t(i,k)\big),
\]
where $\sigma$ is logistic or isotonic, $a,b>0$ fitted on labeled data (``is company $i$ truly tied to theme $k$?''). Optionally blend in auxiliary channels (set coefficients to zero if unused):
\[
\tilde{\ell}_t(i,k) \;=\; \sigma\!\left(\theta_d\,\ell_t(i,k) + \theta_R \hat{R}_{i,k} + \theta_T \hat{T}_{i,k} + \theta_S \hat{S}_{i,k} + \cdots \right),\quad \theta_\bullet \ge 0.
\]

\paragraph{Primary Assignment.} For mutual exclusivity when needed:
\[
k^\star(i,t)=\arg\max_k \tilde{\ell}_t(i,k)\quad \text{if}\ \tilde{\ell}_t(i,(1))\ge \tau_1\ \land\ \tilde{\ell}_t(i,(1))-\tilde{\ell}_t(i,(2))\ge \Delta,
\]
else assign to the L1 of the top theme with ``diversified'' flag. Apply 2-quarter hysteresis to reduce churn.

\section{Index Construction from the KG}

\subsection{Universe}
For each theme $k$ at time $t$:
\[
\text{Base Universe} \;=\; \mathcal{U}_k(t;\tau_d, L_{\max}) \cap \{ \text{float-adj mkt cap}>\$500\text{m},\ \text{ADV}_{90d}>\$2\text{m} \}.
\]

\subsection{Selection}
Target 30--60 names:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item Include all names in top decile of $\tilde{\ell}_t(i,k)$ within the base universe.
\item Add by descending $\tilde{\ell}_t(i,k)$ until target count is reached, subject to liquidity/country caps and buffer rules.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Weights}
Purity-tilted cap weighting:
\[
w_i \propto \mathrm{Cap}_i^{\alpha} \cdot \tilde{\ell}_t(i,k)^{\beta},\qquad \alpha\in[0,1],\ \beta\ge 0,
\]
with constraints: single name $\le 8\%$ (or 5\% UCITS), country $\le 25\%$, related-party $=0$. Rebalance quarterly; fast-entry for eligible IPOs/spin-offs.

\section{Implementation Notes}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Source weighting $q_{\text{src}}$:} tier reputable outlets/filings higher than secondary aggregation; cap per-source contribution to avoid bias.
\item \textbf{Relation penalties $\Omega(r)$:} e.g., $\Omega(\texttt{about})=0$, $\Omega(\texttt{supplies\_to})=0.1$, $\Omega(\texttt{partner\_with})=0.2$, $\Omega(\texttt{mentions})=0.6$.
\item \textbf{Admissible meta-paths $\Pi$ (examples):} $C\!\to\!T$ (asserted align), $C\!\to\!N\!\to\!T$, $C\!\to\!C\_{\text{supplies}}\!\to\!N\!\to\!T$, $C\!\to\!P\!\to\!T$ where $P$ is Product/Technology.
\item \textbf{Schema governance:} L1 theme set is controlled (enum). L2/L3 expandable with parent pointers. Maintain alias/ID maps for companies (PERMIDs/LEIs) and securities (ISIN, MIC, ticker).
\item \textbf{Extraction QA:} require multi-source corroboration or a minimum path multiplicity before inclusion; log provenance spans for audit.
\end{itemize}

\section{Appendix: Minimal Prompt Skeleton for Triple Extraction}

You are extracting financial knowledge graph triples.

SCHEMA:
- Entity types: [Theme_L1 (enum: ...), Theme_L2, Company, Security, Person,
  Organization, Product, Technology, Event (enum: ...), Document, Geography,
  Facility, Contract, Metric, Asset, Standard]
- Relations: [mentions, about, reports_metric(value,unit,period),
  has_event(type), located_in, operates, produces, uses_technology,
  supplies_to, customer_of, partner_with, competes_with, subsidiary_of,
  acquires, invests_in, awarded_contract, contract_with, contract_scope,
  aligns_to_theme, enables_theme, revenue_from_theme, event_affects,
  regulates, approves/denies, fines/sues, publishes, speaks_at, appointed,
  guides, indexed_in]

TASK:
- From the text, emit JSON list of triples:
  {head: {name, type}, relation, tail: {name, type}, time, source_url,
   confidence, extras:{...}}.
- Use Theme_L1 names exactly as enumerated.
- Prefer 'about' over 'mentions' when applicable.
- Include Metric values with unit and period if reported.
- Include evidence_span (quote) where possible.
